(a) 
Pros:  
- Allows teams to adjust to changing requirements and priorities quickly
- Frequent testing and feedback reduces undiscovered issues and improves the quality of the end product
Cons: 
- Project scope and timeline can be hard to estimate
- Large projects with complex dependencies may struggle with iterative development

(b)
The scrum master manages the scrum processes and works towards achieving agile development. They fix issues the development team has and 
remove distractions. The product owner represents stakeholders and ensures business value, as well as communicating visions and requirements to the team.
